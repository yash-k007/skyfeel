Welcome to Skyfeel ☁️

Log how you feel in a few seconds. Skyfeel attaches the day's weather so you can discover your patterns.

  • 🔒 Your entries never leave this phone. No account, no server, no tracking.
  • 🌍 To get the weather, only an approximate location (about 1 km) is sent to Open-Meteo. Never your mood or notes.
  • 🗑️ You can export or delete everything at any time in Settings.

Skyfeel is a personal journal, not a medical tool.
