Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

The short version

Skyfeel does not collect your data. Your mood entries, tags and notes are stored only on your own device. The maker of Skyfeel cannot see them, and neither can anyone else.

What is stored, and where

Your entries (date, mood, tags, note, and the day's weather), your chosen location and your reminder time are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server run by Skyfeel. Skyfeel has no server, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies.

What is shared, and with whom

To show the weather, Skyfeel requests forecasts from Open-Meteo. These requests contain only an approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) or the name of the city you search for. Like any website, Open-Meteo can see your device's IP address when it answers. Your moods, tags and notes are never sent. See Open-Meteo's own terms and privacy policy.

If you choose "Use my location", your device asks your permission first. You can instead type a city, or use the app with no location at all.

Your control

  • Export all your entries at any time (Settings → Your data).
  • Delete a single entry, or everything, at any time. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data also erases everything.

Your rights (GDPR)

Because Skyfeel never receives your personal data, it holds nothing it could give you access to, correct or erase on your behalf: you hold all of it yourself, on your device, and the controls above let you exercise those rights directly.

Not a medical tool

Skyfeel is a personal journal for curiosity and self-reflection. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you are struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a health professional.

Children

Skyfeel is not designed for children under 14.
